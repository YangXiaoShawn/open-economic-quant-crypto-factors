# Data Access & Provenance

Raw exchange archives are not redistributed in this repository; aggregated result tables live under `data/results/` and `reports/`.

## Public Sources

1. **Binance Public Data Dumps** (`data.binance.vision`): Spot and USDT-margined perpetual 1-minute klines (with taker-buy volume), monthly funding rates, and daily open-interest / long-short ratio series across 105 coins (2024-06 through 2026-09).
2. **OKX Public Market Data** (via `ccxt`): Daily OHLCV and funding rates for cross-exchange replication.
