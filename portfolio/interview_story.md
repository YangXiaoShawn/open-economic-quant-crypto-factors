# Interview Story: Equity Microstructure Factors in Crypto

- **Question**: Do equity microstructure factors built for A-shares transfer to crypto where minute bars, taker flow, and perpetual-futures positioning are openly available?
- **Finding**: Most A-share order-flow and momentum factors fail or prove parameter-fragile across 730 days; the signals that hold up are crypto-native perpetual **funding rates** (Sharpe +1.42, 91% of rolling windows positive, confirmed on OKX), **retail long/short positioning** (+1.39), and **low-price range** (+1.63).
- **Post-Publication Honesty**: Tracked out of sample after the May 2026 cutoff without re-fitting, the market-neutral composite returned +4.6% in June, -6.9% in July, and -4.0% in August (-6.5% over 92 days) as altcoin short-leg squeezes hit the funding and range factors.
