# Resume Bullets

- Evaluated 32 equity microstructure factors plus crypto-native derivatives signals across 105 coins over 730 days on Binance and OKX, demonstrating that short 120-day samples manufacture spurious Sharpe > 3.0 factors that turn negative over full cycles.
- Built a frozen post-publication out-of-sample audit (June-September 2026) decomposition showing 83% of the funding factor return comes from price reversal and 17% from carry, while documenting post-publication regime drawdowns (-6.5% over 92 days) without re-fitting.
